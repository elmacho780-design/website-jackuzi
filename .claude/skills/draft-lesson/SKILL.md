Draft lesson $ARGUMENTS. Read HANDOFF.md first if you have not this session.

1. Look up the lesson in `reference/HML_Lesson_Library.xlsx`, sheet `04 Lesson library`: code, level, English title, what it teaches. Check which lessons come before it in the course (HANDOFF.md section 5) so it builds on them and does not repeat them.
2. Read `reference/teaching-guide/presenter-guide-voiceover.md` sections "The slide sequence", "Teaching principles", "Voiceover rules", "Closing call to action" and "Pronunciation", and read two finished lessons in `reference/teaching-guide/slide-text-lessons-1-10.md` as models.
3. Copy `drafts/_TEMPLATE.md` to `drafts/<CODE>.md` and fill it in: every slide's English with its Mongolian directly under, the build order, what the picture shows, the layout to use (see CLAUDE.md), and the voiceover guide (Say lines with [click] marks, a teaching note, a timing). About 10 slides, 6 to 7 minutes.
4. Rules: examples before the grammar term; one idea per sentence; A1 vocabulary only; the Анхаар! slide is the mistake Mongolian speakers really make, with the Mongolian reason; the model text uses everything taught; British English; no em dashes; never name an exam, a course, "last lesson" or "next lesson"; nothing about quizzes.
5. Mongolian: write natural, simple Mongolian, keep ө and ү correct, and keep "дүрэм" for grammar. Mark the file `Status: DRAFT (Mongolian not checked)`. Never present your Mongolian as final.
6. Stop there. Show Battumur the draft and ask him to approve or correct it. Do not build slides until he approves.
