# Part 1 · Master rules

## 1. Your job

You are rebuilding a video lesson deck for **HML Academy**, an English school in Ulaanbaatar, Mongolia. The deck you are working in, **GR-007 have got**, is the approved design. Keep its look exactly: the fonts, colours, sizes, backgrounds, logo, cards, tables and spacing. Change only the content, slide by slide, using the lesson in Part 2. The new deck must look like the next deck in the same series as GR-007.

The students are Mongolian beginners (CEFR A1), mostly teenagers, watching a narrated video on a phone. The slides are the only thing they see. They cannot yet read English on their own, so every English line has its Mongolian meaning directly under it.

## 2. Never do these

Each rule below fixes a real mistake from our earlier decks.

1. **Never retype, re-spell, translate or correct Mongolian.** Copy every Mongolian line from Part 2 exactly, letter for letter.
2. **Mongolian has two letters that Russian does not: ү and ө.** Never change ү to у. Never change ө to е or о. Wrong: *Хунийг дурслэх, зев, еглеений*. Right: *Хүнийг дүрслэх, зөв, өглөөний*.
3. **English words inside a Mongolian line stay in English letters**, exactly as written. Wrong: *апу*. Right: *any*.
4. **All text is live, editable text.** Never put text inside a generated picture. Never generate a whole slide as a picture. No ghost text, faded words or watermarks in any background.
5. **Never write instructions on a slide.** The words IMAGE, Card 1, Left column, Note bar, EN and MN, and every description of a picture, are instructions only.
6. **Never add letters, words or symbols that are not in the lesson text.** No stray *A*, no *^*, no labels such as *BOY* or *APPLE*.
7. **Nothing overlaps.** Text stays fully inside its card with 32 px of space on every side. A label chip sits above the text, never on top of it. The logo never touches a title.
8. **Never shrink text to fit.** Use the exact sizes in section 4. If text does not fit, make the card taller or let the line wrap.
9. **The same kind of text is the same size on every slide.**
10. **Never add, remove, merge or reorder slides.**
11. **No em dashes anywhere.**

## 3. Colours

- Content slide background: warm off-white **#F8F6F2**.
- Title and Remember slide background: deep navy **#0F1F3D**.
- English text, titles, header chips and table headers: navy **#1F2B4A**.
- Every Mongolian line on a light slide: maroon **#8A2433**.
- Target words: bold blue **#2B6CB0**.
- Cards: light grey **#EFEFEF**. Highlight cells: light blue **#DCE6F4**.
- Note bars and the Watch out frame: soft pink **#F8EAEC** with a 3 px maroon **#8A2433** border.
- Correct: green **#2E7D32**. Mistakes: red **#B23A48** with a line through.
- On navy slides: English white **#FFFFFF**; Mongolian light blue **#8FB6F2** on the Title slide and pink **#E39AAB** on the Remember slide; number circles light blue **#B7D0F5**.

## 4. Fonts and sizes

Two fonts only. Both contain ү and ө.

- **Roboto Condensed Bold** for slide titles and the big title on the Title slide.
- **Roboto Bold** for all other text. **Roboto Regular** for the footer only.

Sizes are Canva font sizes on a 1920 × 1080 slide. Never change them, never auto-fit.

- **Title slide:** English title 170 (if the title is longer than 14 letters, 120 on up to two lines). Mongolian title 80. Code and level chips 40. Promise lines 40.
- **Content slide title:** English 100. Mongolian subtitle 64.
- **Card text:** English 44. Mongolian 40.
- **Story and model text lines:** English 40. Mongolian 36.
- **Label chips:** 30, English on the first line, Mongolian on the second.
- **Tables:** header row 34. Cells: English 42, Mongolian under it 34.
- **Note bar:** English 40. Mongolian 38.
- **Watch out:** context English 34, Mongolian 32. Sentences 40. Mongolian 34.
- **Remember slide:** title 120, Mongolian 72. Card English 42, card Mongolian 40. Numbers 80.
- **Footer:** 30.

## 5. Logo and footer

- **Logo:** the HML Higher Mind logo on its white rounded tile, exactly as in GR-007. Content slides: top right. Title and Remember slides: top left. Keep at least 40 px between the tile and any text. On the Remember slide the title starts to the right of the tile.
- **Footer, bottom right:** on the Title slide, *Curated by Battumur.D*. On every other slide, the footer line given in the lesson, for example *Lesson 1 · GR-003 · Personal pronouns*.

## 6. Layouts

Every slide in Part 2 names one layout. Each layout copies one slide of GR-007. Keep that slide's structure and change only the text and pictures.

- **TITLE:** copy GR-007 slide 1.
- **STORY:** copy GR-007 slide 2. Picture on the left half. Up to 6 lines on the right, each English line with its Mongolian line under it. In a dialogue, the speaker's name is bold navy at the start of the English line.
- **CARDS-4:** copy GR-007 slide 3. Four cards in a 2 × 2 grid. Optional label chip at the top left of each card, optional icon on the left, text on the right. If the slide also has a note bar, make the cards shorter and put the bar underneath.
- **TABLE:** copy GR-007 slide 4. Navy header row (English on top, Mongolian under it), light rows, up to two note bars under the table. Up to 7 rows; all rows the same height.
- **CARDS-3:** copy GR-007 slide 5. Three equal cards in a row, then a note bar.
- **YES-NO:** copy GR-007 slide 6. Three columns: Question, Yes, No.
- **TWO-COLUMNS:** copy GR-007 slide 7. Two equal columns, each with a navy header chip and a light card under it, then a note bar across the bottom.
- **WATCH-OUT:** copy GR-007 slide 8. Pink frame, warning icon and context lines at the top. Numbered rows: red ✗ box with the wrong sentence, green ✓ box with the right sentence, and the Mongolian of the right sentence on the far right.
- **MODEL:** copy GR-007 slide 9. Picture on the left, up to 6 lines on the right. With 7 or 8 lines, remove the picture and set the lines in two columns.
- **REMEMBER:** copy GR-007 slide 10. Three numbered cards.
- **CARDS-5** (lesson GR-016 only): five equal narrow cards in one row, a picture at the top of each, the word and its Mongolian under it.

Inside every card or cell: English first, Mongolian directly under it. Left-aligned in cards; centred in table cells and chips. Cards in the same row are the same size, with equal space between them.

## 7. How to read Part 2

- **EN:** English text. **MN:** its Mongolian, placed directly under the EN line, in the same card.
- **\[\[word\]\]:** target word. Show it bold blue #2B6CB0 and remove the brackets. On Watch out slides, \[\[word\]\] in the right sentence is bold green #2E7D32.
- **{{word}}:** the mistake. Show it red #B23A48 with a line through it and remove the braces.
- **IMAGE:** a picture to generate and place. Never write this line on the slide.
- **Note bar:** a pink box (section 3) holding the EN line with the MN line under it. Table rows: cells are separated by |. Inside a cell or a short list, Mongolian in round brackets goes on its own line under the English before it, maroon, without the brackets. Text after MN: is always copied exactly as written, brackets included.

## 8. Pictures

- Every illustration matches the Nomin and best-friend pictures in GR-007: friendly flat vector style, soft shading, Mongolian characters with warm skin tones and black hair, clothes in navy, maroon and white, plain off-white background.
- Icons match GR-007 slide 3: simple navy line icons.
- Pictures never contain text, letters or numbers.
- Image prompt to use in Canva: *Friendly flat vector illustration, soft shading, \[the IMAGE description\], Mongolian people with warm skin tones and black hair, clothes in navy, maroon and white, plain off-white background, no text, no letters, no numbers.*

## 9. Final check

Go through every slide and fix anything that fails.

- [ ] Every Mongolian line matches Part 2 letter for letter, with ү and ө wherever Part 2 has them.
- [ ] All text is editable. No text inside pictures. No instruction words on any slide.
- [ ] The same kind of text is the same size on every slide.
- [ ] Nothing overlaps and nothing sits outside its card.
- [ ] Target words are blue; mistakes are red and struck through; corrections are green.
- [ ] Logo and footer on every slide, in the same place.
- [ ] Exactly the slides listed, in order.
